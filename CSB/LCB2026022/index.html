1. Famous Ghats of VARANASI

Varanasi has 84–88 ghats along the Ganges, including major ones like Dashashwamedh, Assi, Manikarnika, and Harishchandra, each serving distinct ritual, cultural, and historical purposes. These riverfront steps form a 6.5 km sacred waterfront central to bathing, worship, cremation, and daily life in the city.

The ghats of Varanasi are steps leading down to the River Ganga. They are important places for prayer, religious ceremonies, and daily activities. The ghats offer beautiful views of the river and reflect the spiritual life of the city.

More About Varanasi Ghats

Dashashwamedh Ghat is one of the most famous ghats in Varanasi. It is well known for the beautiful Ganga Aarti performed every evening, when priests offer prayers to the River Ganga with lamps and chants. Assi Ghat is another popular place where visitors enjoy the peaceful riverside atmosphere, morning prayers, and sunrise views.

Manikarnika Ghat is known for its traditional Hindu cremation rituals and its religious significance. Visitors can also take a boat ride on the River Ganga to see the ghats and the city's beautiful architecture from the water. These ghats represent the history, faith, and traditions of Varanasi.

Highlights of Varanasi Ghats

  • Sunrise and boat rides at Assi Ghat

2. Famous Temples of VARANASI

Varanasi is famous for its ancient temples, which attract devotees and tourists throughout the year. These temples are important centres of faith, history, and traditional Indian architecture.

More about varanasi temples

Kashi Vishwanath Temple is one of the most important temples in Varanasi and is dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is a major pilgrimage site for Hindus. Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple is dedicated to Lord Hanuman and is visited by many devotees seeking blessings. Durga Temple, also known as the Monkey Temple, is dedicated to Goddess Durga and is known for its distinctive red appearance. Annapurna Devi Temple is dedicated to Goddess Annapurna, who is associated with food and nourishment. These temples reflect the religious importance and cultural heritage of Varanasi. Visitors can explore their architecture, learn about local traditions, and experience the devotional atmosphere of the city.

Famous temples to visit

  1. Kashi Vishwanath Temple
  2. Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple
  3. Durga Temple
  4. Annapurna Devi Temple
  5. New Vishwanath Temple at Banaras Hindu University

3. Culture & Tradition of VARANASI

Varanasi has a rich cultural heritage and is known for its religious festivals, classical music, traditional silk sarees, and local food. The evening Ganga Aarti is one of the city's most memorable experiences. Visitors can explore the narrow streets, taste famous Banarasi paan and local sweets, and observe traditional ceremonies along the river. The city's combination of ancient temples, sacred ghats, and living traditions makes it a unique destination in India.
